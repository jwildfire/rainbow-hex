The rule of the rainbow hex: the spectrum is only seen through hexes or as a thin edge. It never colours text and never fills a plain area.

Rules

  • The spectrum only through hexes or as a thin edge. One band a page, a strip or two as dividers.
  • No gradients on text. No emoji as icons.
  • One colour per page or section (--dept), plus green for good, amber for a look, red for bad.
  • Start compact; put detail behind a click.
  • Works at phone width and in the dark. No scripts.
